Background gallery

Add as many images or short looping videos as you like — wallpapers, art, anything you have the rights to use. Click one to set it as your background, hit shuffle for a random pick, or turn on auto-random below. Images are backed up in your export/ pendrive JSON; videos are kept in this browser's local video storage instead (too large for that format) — worth keeping a copy of the original video file separately. (I can't pull media straight from wallpaper sites like moewalls.com — they don't offer a way to fetch programmatically, and much of that art is copyrighted anime character work I can't source or redistribute myself. Download what you like there for your own use, then add it here.)

Images: keep under ~4MB. Videos: any size works, but very large files take longer to load the first time.
